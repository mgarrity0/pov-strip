    Strip over USB

    The ticked slides go straight into the ESP32's flash over the USB cable. No recompiling: the firmware stays as it is and plays what you send.

    not connected
    Firmware only: chip, pins, board. First time or after changing those; 1 to 5 minutes. Pictures are not part of it: tick slides and use Send.
    Live on the stripconnect to tune
    Takes effect as you drag, no re-upload. Sweep speed is how fast the strip crosses your eye: slower than the speed the slides were made for stretches each pass and the gap so the picture keeps its width. A head shake of ±8° at 2 Hz peaks near 100°/s; a 25° glance runs 300 to 500°/s. Gap at 0 = as uploaded (scaled with the speed). Current limit dims an image whose brightest column would draw more than this; 0.4 A is the ceiling for a strip fed from the board's USB, raise it to your supply's rating once the strip has its own 5 V. 0 = off. Apply or Send saves these on the strip.
    Plug the ESP32 in, pick its port, Connect.

    Export to firmware

    The header holds the ticked slides, resampled for this strip, plus the column period, gap and direction. The project zip is the whole sketch with config.h set from these controls: unzip and open pov_strip/pov_strip.ino in Arduino IDE.
